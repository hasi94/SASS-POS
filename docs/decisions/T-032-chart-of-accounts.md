# T-032 decisions: chart of accounts, fiscal periods, period closing

Autonomous team-mode decisions by `ba` for `docs/specs/T-032-chart-of-accounts.md`. Each is the most conventional, safest, reversible choice. Format: `D-n | Question | Decision | Reason | Reversible`.

D-1 | What does FP-260 build? | Three tables: `accounts`, `fiscal_years`, `fiscal_periods`, plus the five owed columns and their foreign keys. Not built: `account_mappings` (FP-262), `account_balances` (FP-261/FP-268, maintained at posting), `financial_accounts` (FP-263), `currencies`/`exchange_rates`. | The backlog row names chart of accounts, fiscal periods and period closing; the neighbouring rows own the rest. | Reversible: yes, each is an additive table later.

D-2 | Which columns does the owed work add? | Five: `products.income_account_id`, `cogs_account_id`, `inventory_account_id`, `categories.income_account_id`, `customers.receivable_account_id`. `categories` gets only `income_account_id` because `docs/database.md` §6.3 lists no other account column there. Expand migration (column) and a separate contract migration (FK, `RESTRICT`, with a data-clean pre-check) per pair. | T-027 D-3/D-4 and T-028 D-19: an FK onto an existing table is always contract phase. Nullable, no default, no backfill (nothing documented says NOT NULL). | Reversible: yes.

D-3 | Which `control_type` values are single, which repeatable? | Single live account: ar, ap, grni, payroll_payable, retained_earnings. Repeatable: none, cash, bank, inventory, tax_payable, tax_receivable. | The design says "unique per control_type where it must be single" without a list. The five single ones are the defaults posting rules (§7) fall back on with exactly one answer; cash, bank, inventory and tax legitimately vary by location, bank and per-product override. A soft-deleted holder does not block. | Reversible: yes, relaxing a single type is additive.

D-4 | Period transition model. | Adjacent moves only (open to soft_closed to closed, and back one step), an `expected_status` compare-and-set, a monotonic rule (earlier periods never lower than later ones), blockers via a port, reopen needs a reason and a separate permission. | `soft_closed` and `closed` are documented; the rest is the conventional safe shape. Skipping straight from open to closed would bypass the soft stage's audit trail. The compare-and-set stops a replayed close from silently succeeding (T-028 D-21 / R-1 class). | Reversible: yes, allowing open to closed is additive.

D-5 | Year-end closing entry and the `closing` state. | Not built. `CloseFiscalYear` requires all periods closed, sets the year `closed`, leaves `retained_earnings_entry_id` NULL, never uses `closing`. | The entry needs journal entries (FP-261) and no row owns the roll-up; building a state with no worker would strand a year. Flagged as open question 1. | Reversible: yes.

D-6 | What is the seeded chart? | `Refference/ERP.md` §11's list (1000 Assets, 1100 Cash, 1200 Bank, 1300 Inventory, 2000 Liabilities, 2100 Supplier Payables, 2200 Loans, 3000 Equity, 4000 Revenue, 4100 Sales, 5000 Cost of Goods Sold, 6000 Expenses, 6100 Rent, 6200 Salary, 6300 Electricity) plus the control accounts the documented posting rules need: 1400 Accounts Receivable (ar), 2300 Tax Payable (tax_payable), 2400 Goods Received Not Invoiced (grni), 2500 Payroll Payable (payroll_payable), 3100 Retained Earnings (retained_earnings). Headers: 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 6000. 1100 cash, 1200 bank, 1300 inventory, 2100 ap are control accounts under their roots; 5000 is a postable leaf (cogs). 1100, 1200, 1300, 1400 under 1000; 2100 to 2500 under 2000; 3100 under 3000; 4100 under 4000; 6100 to 6300 under 6000. Every control-type account is `is_system`. No tax_receivable account is seeded. | ERP.md lists only a minimum; the database design names the control types and says the seed follows ERP.md §11. Adding exactly the five missing control accounts makes the single-control rules satisfiable. Codes are an invention beyond ERP.md and are marked so. | Reversible: yes for non-system rows; system codes are fixed once data posts, so the owner should review before FP-261 ships.

D-7 | How does Finance know whether an account is used when the journal and other modules do not exist yet? | Three published ports (account usage, account referrers, period-close blockers) plus a fiscal-year usage port, defaulting to "none", implemented later by FP-261 and by the catalogue and customer packages. | Modules may not read each other's tables (boundary rule); the defaults are the only honest answer today. The risk (guards inert until bound) is stated as a forward dependency, with a test using a bound fake. | Reversible: yes.

D-8 | Fiscal year shape and how the first year is created. | Twelve calendar-month periods; start on the first of a month; each new year starts the day after the last; the first may start on any first-of-month. Provisioning (and an idempotent migration for existing tenants) creates the current calendar year, 1 January to 31 December. | `company_profile.fiscal_year_start_month` is not built (T-028 D-18). The gate fails closed with no year, so a new tenant would refuse every sale posting without one. 1 January is the conventional default. | Reversible: yes before the first posting; after that a wrong year is a correction, not an edit.

D-9 | Do `accounts.opening_balance`, `opening_balance_date` and `currency` take input? | No. Columns are built nullable but a request supplying them (or a non-null currency) is refused whole. Opening balances belong to FP-261's `opening` entries; currency needs `currencies`/`exchange_rates`. | A balance column beside a ledger is two sources of truth (T-028 D-2/D-28). No decimal input in this task also removes the width and precision attack surface the T-028 review found (W-6). | Reversible: yes, additive later.

D-10 | Are unknown or forbidden input fields ignored? | Refused whole-request, naming each. | T-028 D-8 precedent: silent ignoring lets a caller believe something was set. | Reversible: yes.

D-11 | NOT NULL tightening on the five owed columns. | Deferred; none is introduced and none is documented. | T-008 D-10: the planner refuses a destructive migration when backups are unavailable, which would break provisioning. A NOT NULL add is not destructive under D-10's own text, but nothing requires it and a later tighten must pass the planner then. | Reversible: yes.

D-12 | Hierarchy limits. | Maximum depth 10 levels (depth 0 to 9). `path` is ids joined by `/`, so recoding never rewrites it, and 10 levels is about 270 characters, inside `VARCHAR(500)`. Parent must be a header of the same type; control accounts are leaves. | Documented columns `path`, `depth`, `is_postable` ("only leaves take postings") imply it; the number is a safe bound with headroom. | Reversible: yes, raising the limit is additive.

D-13 | Account code format and collation bypass. | Upper-case ASCII letters and digits, `.` and `-` inside; whitespace refused (not trimmed); lower case upper-cased on write; uniqueness case-insensitive and across soft-deleted rows. Enums accept only the exact lower-case values. | The database's default collations compare case-insensitively and pad-insensitively, so `ab1`, `AB1` and `AB1 ` would otherwise collide or slip past (T-008 D-17 reasoning; T-028 review class). | Reversible: yes, loosening is additive.

D-14 | Deactivate or delete an account referenced by products, categories or customers. | Refused while any live record references it (deactivate) or any record references it (delete); nothing is cascaded. | A soft delete does not trip the foreign key, so the check must be explicit; dead-account references would fail later at posting time instead of now. | Reversible: yes.

D-15 | Permission split. | Separate keys for manage, close, reopen and post-adjustment, as in AC-64. | Reopening a closed period is the highest-risk action in this task; it must be grantable apart from closing. | Reversible: yes.

D-16 | Recoding and retyping accounts with postings. | Refused once the account has postings (via port); allowed before. | Reports snapshot codes and the ledger's meaning depends on type. | Reversible: yes.

D-17 | Reason for being conservative on year deletion. | Only the latest year, all periods open, no entries. | Deleting a middle year would break the contiguity rule (AC-33). | Reversible: yes.

D-18 | Merge order with T-029. | Not decided here; the spec states the rule (second to merge rebases, re-sorts migration names, regenerates the catalogue list) and leaves the order to the lead. | Both tasks edit the products migration set, handlers and schema catalogue; the columns differ. | Reversible: yes.

D-19 | Can section I (owed columns) be built on this branch? | No until T-027 and T-028 are on the branch (`main` stops at T-013). Spec splits the work: sections A to H and J independent; section I a second pass after rebase. | Measured from the lead's brief: the target tables are absent on the base. | Reversible: yes.
